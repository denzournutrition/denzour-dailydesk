INSIDE SALES - how it works

SET UP (once)
1. People > edit each sales person > Designation = "Inside Sales". They now see "Sales desk" in the menu.
2. Admin menu > Sales targets > pick the month > enter the TEAM sales target (total for the month).
   Optional: orders target, calls per person per day, and a different target for any person.
   Empty person boxes = the team target is split equally.
   Do this on the 1st of each month.

EVERY DAY (sales person)
1. Sales desk > Upload your sheet > choose the Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file > Read file.
2. Check the list it shows (orders tabs and call tabs are found automatically) > Import selected tabs.
3. Type the call minutes for the day in "Day by day" > Save call minutes.
   Their numbers, target progress and the CEO dashboard update straight away.
   Uploading the same file again does not double anything. "Undo" removes a whole upload.

WHAT IT READS
- Order tabs: need the columns "Shopify Order Id" and "Final Amount" (Date, Contact No., Name, Source, Product, Code, Payment, Dispatch, RTO as in your Sales Track Report).
- Call tabs: need "Contact No." and "Talk Remarks" (plus Date and Name).
- Other tabs are ignored. Title rows, blank rows, and different date styles are handled.
- A call counts as "connected" unless the remark says things like not responding, ringing, not reachable, switched off, disconnected.
- "Orders after a call" = orders whose phone number was already in the call log on or before the order date.
- Every order in an uploaded file is credited to the person who uploads it. An order already uploaded by someone else is skipped, so two people cannot both claim it.

WHO SEES WHAT
- Sales person: only their own Sales desk.
- CEO / admin / anyone with dashboard access: "Inside Sales" dashboard with the team target, achieved so far, pace, leaderboard, sources, coupon codes.
- Admin: also sets targets.

LIMITS
- Upload size is limited by your host (usually 20 MB or more). If a big file fails, upload only the needed tabs as CSV.
- Call minutes are typed by hand. A dialer export can be added later.

WHO CAN UPLOAD (v8)
- People > open a person > tick "Can upload sales sheets (Sales desk)" > Save changes.
  That person then sees "Sales desk" in the menu. Designation does not matter any more.
- People with designation "Inside Sales" have it automatically.
- Admin: Sales desk > pick the sales person at the top > upload their sheet for them.
